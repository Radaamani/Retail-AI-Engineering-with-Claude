# 1. Retail/CPG Sales Performance Analysis & Executive Insights Generation

**Business Problem Statement:** Retail and CPG organizations generate large volumes of sales, promotion, pricing, inventory, and market-share data across multiple brands, products, channels, and geographies. Business leaders often spend significant time identifying performance drivers, understanding revenue variances, and preparing executive summaries for decision-making.

**Sample Business Scenario:** A national beverage company observes that Q2 sales revenue declined by 8% despite a 12% increase in promotional spending.

**Representative Dataset (Masked Sample):**

| **Region** | **Product** | **Sales Volume** | **Revenue (\$)** | **Promotion Spend (\$)** | **Inventory** |
|----|----|----|----|----|----|
| North | Product A | 24,500 | 980,000 | 120,000 | 5,000 |
| South | Product A | 18,000 | 720,000 | 90,000 | 8,500 |
| West | Product B | 15,500 | 620,000 | 60,000 | 12,000 |

**Key Business Metrics to Monitor:**

- Revenue Growth %

- Sales Volume Growth %

- Gross Margin %

- Market Share %

- Promotion ROI

- Inventory Turnover

- Out-of-Stock Rate

- Average Selling Price (ASP)

**Potential Performance Drivers:**

- Ineffective promotional campaigns

- Regional demand shifts

- Pricing changes

- Competitor activities

- Product availability issues

- Distribution gaps

- Seasonal buying patterns

**GenAI Activities:** Participants can use Claude to:

- Analyze sales data and identify trends/anomalies

- Explain revenue increases or declines

- Identify top-performing and underperforming products/regions

- Generate executive-ready business summaries

- Recommend corrective actions

- Create presentation-ready narratives

**Expected Business Outcomes:**

- Faster insight generation

- Improved decision-making

- Reduction in manual reporting effort

- Enhanced executive storytelling

- Data-backed recommendations for growth opportunities

# 2. Supply Chain Demand Planning & Inventory Optimization Assistant

**Business Problem Statement:** Supply chain teams must balance inventory availability while avoiding excess stock. Demand fluctuations, supplier constraints, and inaccurate forecasts can lead to stockouts, increased carrying costs, and customer dissatisfaction.

**Sample Business Scenario:** A retailer forecasts monthly demand of 10,000 units for a product, but due to a seasonal promotion actual demand reaches 15,000 units, resulting in stockouts across multiple regions and lost sales opportunities.

**Examples of Demand Fluctuations:**

- Seasonal events and festivals

- Promotional campaigns

- New product launches

- Weather-related demand spikes

- Competitor disruptions

- Market trend changes

**Representative Dataset (Masked Sample):**

| **Product** | **Forecast Demand** | **Actual Demand** | **Inventory Available** | **Safety Stock** | **Lead Time (Days)** |
|----|----|----|----|----|----|
| Product X | 10,000 | 15,000 | 4,500 | 2,000 | 14 |
| Product Y | 8,000 | 6,500 | 12,000 | 1,500 | 10 |
| Product Z | 5,000 | 5,800 | 3,000 | 1,000 | 21 |

**Common High-Severity Risks:**

- Inventory stockouts

- Excess inventory/overstock

- Supplier delivery delays

- Forecast inaccuracies

- Slow-moving inventory

- Lost sales due to unavailable stock

- Increased warehousing costs

**Key Supply Chain Metrics:**

- Forecast Accuracy %

- Inventory Turnover Ratio

- Fill Rate %

- Service Level %

- Days of Inventory on Hand (DOH)

- Stockout Frequency

- Order Fulfillment Rate

- Inventory Carrying Cost

**Potential Optimization Actions:**

- Adjust safety stock levels

- Rebalance inventory across locations

- Prioritize replenishment for high-demand products

- Reduce excess inventory through redistribution

- Update forecasting assumptions

- Identify supplier risks and alternative sourcing options

**GenAI Activities:** Participants can use Claude to:

- Analyze forecast vs actual demand

- Identify inventory risks and supply chain bottlenecks

- Prioritize high-risk SKUs

- Recommend inventory optimization actions

- Generate stakeholder-ready reports and summaries

- Simulate business impact of recommended actions

**Expected Business Outcomes:**

- Improved inventory utilization

- Reduced stockouts and excess inventory

- Better forecast-driven planning

- Faster identification of supply chain risks

- More informed operational decision-making
